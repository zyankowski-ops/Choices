Week 3: Choices on the Job

You've made it through your first two weeks at an automotive repair shop. You're beginning to understand how the shop operates, people are giving you more responsibility, and you're starting to work with less direct supervision.

This isn't a test of automotive knowledge.

You're still inside your 30-day probationary period. During that time, your employer isn't only watching whether you can perform automotive work. They're also learning what kind of employee you are.

Throughout one workday, you'll make decisions involving:

  • time and attendance
  • work habits
  • communication
  • safety
  • quality of work
  • customers and coworkers
  • personal responsibilities

There may not always be an obvious answer.

Some decisions will have two reasonable choices. Other choices may seem minor at the time but become more important when they're combined with the rest of your day.

Make the decision you believe you would actually make on the job.
